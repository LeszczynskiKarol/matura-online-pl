### 6.5. — `aggressive dogs`
Kluczowe: komponent kursu dotyczący agresywnego zachowania psów.
Akceptowane: `aggression in dogs`, `dog/dog's/dogs' aggression`, `dogs full of aggression`, `dogs which are aggressive`, `dogs that have problem(s) with aggression`, `dogs' aggressive behaviour`, `aggressive dog behaviours`, `aggressive behaviour in dogs`, `aggressiveness in dogs`, `hostile/angry dogs`, `aggressive dogs and managing their behaviours`, `dogs to manage their aggression`, `managing aggression in dogs`, `how to manage dog aggression`, `management of aggressive dogs`. <div class="pulapka">
  <div class="pulapka-label">Pułapka — `lever` vs `level`</div>

Klucz CKE wprost klasyfikuje jako 0 pkt: `advance to the next **lever** sooner` (z błędem ortograficznym). `lever` = dźwignia, `level` = poziom. Zmiana jednej litery zmienia znaczenie.
</div>

<div class="pulapka">
  <div class="pulapka-label">Pułapka — `pass` zamiast `take/sit/do`</div>

`take the test / sit the test / do the test` = **przystąpić** do testu (nie zdać). `pass the test` = **zdać** test. Klucz wymaga `pass`, bo kontekst wymaga zdania.
</div>
