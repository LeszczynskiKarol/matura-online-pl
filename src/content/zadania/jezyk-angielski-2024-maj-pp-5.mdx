### 5.4. — `(an) electric bike/bicycle` (z tekstu B)
**Akceptowane**: `(a/an) electric`, `an electrical bike`, `(a/an) electric vehicle / an EV / an e-bike`, `(a) motor bike`, `a bike with electric motor/engine`, `(a/the) electric bike`, `a great/fantastic/cheaper electric bike`, `an ecological bike`, `an electric cycle/two-wheeler`, `a new electric bike`.
**Odrzucone**: `(a) city bike`, `something for her preference`, `bike suitable for my mum`, `an electronic bike`, `FUNBIKE`, `an electrick bike` (błąd ortograficzny), `a much better/good one`, `better/comfortable bike`, `a new bike`, `electric which like new`, `bike electric`, `very an electric bike`, `(an) electric bikes`, `easy to ride`, `my preferences about new bike`, `elektryczny` (zły język), `a bike which will be an electric one` (przekroczony limit wyrazów).

### 5.5. — `fast` / `very fast` (z tekstu D)
**Akceptowane**: `as fast as others/as fast as other bikes/so fast as other types/as fast as different bikes`, `fast as/like other bikes`, `as fast like other bikes`, `(much) faster`, `faster than other bikes`, `quickest on the road`, `(the) fastest (ones)`, `fast compared/comparing to other bikes`, `aren't fast` (powtórzenie wyrazu po luce), `speedy/quick/swift`, `too/really/so/that/as fast`, `riding fast`, `the fastest one`.
**Odrzucone**: `slow / too slow`, `comfortable`, `easy to get on`, `fitted with baskets`, `cheap / safe`, `so expensive`, `for me`, `a good for the planet`, `making as much money`, `as fast as electric bikes`, `smooth / good`, `very popular`, `electric`, `in sport mode`, `fast than other bikes`, `very faster`, `as faster`, `faster like other bikes`, `so quickly`, `as fast as/like other`, `fast too much`, `heavy and fast`, `look like my dream bike`, `speed like other bikes`.

### 5.6. — `box / packaging` (z tekstu A)
**Akceptowane**: `box/packaging with the bike inside`, `box they send`, `box it'll arrive in`, `box that it comes in`, `box containing it`, `shape and size of box`, `shape of the package`, `box is large so it`, `package / parcel`, `the box`, `box where the bike is`, `box it will be in`, `box that contains (the/a) bike`, `box with my bike inside`, `box containing the bike`.
**Odrzucone**: `packet/pack` (sprzeczne z tekstem), `very large strong box`, `box is stronger and it`, `box design`, `coverage box` (uzupełniona niezgodnym elementem), `bike / boxes`, `company introduce new packaging`, `company deliver bikes in boxes`, `boxes where bikes are packed`, `couriers`, `use stronger boxes`, `transport to their clients`, `boxes size and shape`, `box the company will send it in`, `bike will be in the box which`, `box is very large so it` (przekroczony limit wyrazów).

### 5.7. — `the weather is bad` / `there is bad weather` (z tekstu C)
**Akceptowane**: `there are bad weather conditions`, `bad weather conditions appear/occur`, `in bad weather (conditions)`, `it is snowing / it rains a lot`, `anything (bad) happens`, `some problems happen`, `my chain / tyre breaks`, `the tires need changing`, `I am riding in city`, `I ride on the streets`, `the weather is poor / difficult`, `the weather isn't good`, `the weather conditions aren't good / get bad`, `there's a bad weather conditions`, `outside is a bad weather`, `the weather won't be`, `the weather conditions is bad`, `is/it's (a) bad weather`, `are bad weather conditions`, `there is bad weather condition`, `it is bad weather conditions` (akceptowane z błędem językowym).
**Odrzucone**: `it's warm and sunny`, `faced bad weather conditions`, `change tyres`, `bad weather / bad weather conditions` (sam zwrot bez kontekstu, niespójne z otoczeniem luki), `bad weather conditions is`, `it's dark`, `I lost control`, `I service bike in myself`, `change so much`, `its bad weather` (niejasne — bez `it is`), `weather is in bad condition(s)`, `weather is raining`, `my chain or tyre brakes` (błąd ortograficzny zmieniający znaczenie — `brakes` = hamulce, nie `breaks` = pęka).

<div class="pulapka">
  <div class="pulapka-label">Pułapka — `brakes` zamiast `breaks`</div>

Klucz CKE wprost klasyfikuje to jako **błąd zmieniający znaczenie**: `brakes` to rzeczownik (hamulce), `breaks` to czasownik (pęka). Pełna poprawność ortograficzna dla wyrazów wysokiej frekwencji jest wymagana.
</div>

## Punktacja CKE

- **1 pkt** — poprawna odpowiedź (za każde z 5.1.–5.7.).
- **0 pkt** — odpowiedź niepoprawna albo brak odpowiedzi.
- Maksymalnie **7 pkt**.
