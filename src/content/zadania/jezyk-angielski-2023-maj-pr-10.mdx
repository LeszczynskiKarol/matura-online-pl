## Wzorcowa odpowiedź — TEMAT 1 (rozprawka, ≈ 240 wyrazów)

<div class="odpowiedz">
  <div>

**Celebrities in advertising — a marriage made in heaven or a deal with the devil?**

The decision of growing numbers of celebrities, including sports stars and actors, to lend their faces to commercial advertising has provoked heated debate. In my view, this phenomenon brings both substantial benefits and serious drawbacks, and a balanced look at both sides is essential.

On the positive side, celebrity endorsement undeniably works. A famous footballer endorsing a sportswear brand or a beloved actor promoting a charity campaign reaches millions of people far more effectively than traditional advertising. Secondly, celebrities themselves benefit financially — a single endorsement may finance years of training or fund their own foundations. Finally, well-chosen partnerships can promote socially valuable causes: when global stars campaign for environmental protection or mental-health awareness, public attention shifts to issues that might otherwise remain marginal.

However, the disadvantages are equally real. Firstly, celebrities lend credibility to products they may not even use, misleading impressionable young consumers — children seeing their idol drink a sugary beverage assume it must be healthy. Secondly, advertising fees inflate product prices, meaning ordinary customers end up paying for the celebrity's mansion. Furthermore, a celebrity's scandal can instantly tarnish the brand they represent, exposing companies and consumers alike to reputational risk.

To conclude, celebrity advertising is neither inherently good nor bad — its impact depends on whether the partnership is honest, the product worthwhile, and the audience media-literate enough to distinguish endorsement from genuine recommendation. We should welcome socially responsible campaigns and resist exploitative ones.

  </div>
</div>

## Wzorcowa odpowiedź — TEMAT 2 (artykuł, ≈ 235 wyrazów)

<div class="odpowiedz">
  <div>

**The world in 2053: a glimpse into our shared tomorrow**

Imagine waking up in 2053 — what would your morning look like? In the next three decades humanity will witness changes more profound than any since the Industrial Revolution. While one aspect of our lives will undoubtedly improve, another will pose an enormous challenge.

The most significant **improvement** will be **healthcare**. By 2053, personalised medicine — treatments designed around an individual's DNA — will be widely available. Diseases such as Alzheimer's and many cancers will be diagnosed years before symptoms appear, thanks to AI-driven blood tests already in development. The average life expectancy in developed countries may approach 95 years, with people enjoying not just a longer but also a healthier old age. Robotic surgery and wearable diagnostics will turn routine medical care into something as ordinary as brushing teeth.

However, this medical paradise will be overshadowed by humanity's biggest **challenge** — **climate change**. Rising sea levels could displace hundreds of millions of people from coastal megacities, and food production in the tropics may collapse as droughts intensify. Mass migration on an unprecedented scale will strain political systems and could fuel conflicts. Even with current pledges to reach net-zero emissions, the world will struggle to keep warming below the critical 2 °C threshold.

In conclusion, 2053 will be a paradox: we may live longer and healthier lives, but the planet we inhabit will be a far less stable place. Our choices today will define which trend wins.

  </div>
</div>

## Strategia: rozprawka (temat 1)

<div class="krok">
  <div class="krok-label">Krok 1 — teza zapowiadająca strukturę</div>

Teza MUSI odnosić się do **celebrytów w reklamach** ORAZ zapowiadać, że będą rozpatrywane zalety i wady. Bez tej zapowiedzi = max 1 pkt za to kryterium.
</div>

<div class="krok">
  <div class="krok-label">Krok 2 — akapity ZA i PRZECIW</div>

Każdy z 3-4 konkretnymi argumentami + przykładami.
</div>

<div class="krok">
  <div class="krok-label">Krok 3 — konkluzja kompromisowa</div>

Najlepsze konkluzje wskazują warunkowo — „pod warunkiem, że...". Unikaj sztampowych „has both pros and cons".
</div>

## Strategia: artykuł (temat 2)

<div class="krok">
  <div class="krok-label">Krok 1 — tytuł + chwytliwy wstęp</div>

Artykuł MUSI być zatytułowany. Wstęp powinien przyciągać — pytanie retoryczne, anegdota, surprising fact.
</div>

<div class="krok">
  <div class="krok-label">Krok 2 — DWA elementy</div>

Temat ma DWA człony: (a) co zmieni się **na lepsze**, (b) jaki będzie **największy problem**. OBA musisz omówić. Pominięcie jednego = max 2-3 pkt zamiast 5 za treść.
</div>

<div class="krok">
  <div class="krok-label">Krok 3 — Future tenses</div>

`will`, `is going to`, `is likely to`, `may`, `could`, `might`. Wyrażaj **pewność i przypuszczenia** dotyczące przyszłości (V.9).
</div>

## Najczęstsze błędy uczniów

<div class="pulapka">
  <div class="pulapka-label">Pułapka 1 — pominięcie drugiego elementu artykułu</div>

Artykuł o świecie za 30 lat MA DWA elementy: (a) zmiana na lepsze + (b) największe wyzwanie. Pominięcie jednego = max 2/5 pkt za treść. Klucz CKE wprost.
</div>

<div class="pulapka">
  <div class="pulapka-label">Pułapka 2 — rozprawka jako artykuł albo odwrotnie</div>

Klucz CKE: „Jeśli zdający tworzy wypowiedź w innej formie niż wskazana w temacie (np. pisze list zamiast rozprawki), praca jest kwalifikowana we wszystkich elementach formy na poziom 0."
</div>

<div class="pulapka">
  <div class="pulapka-label">Pułapka 3 — artykuł bez tytułu</div>

Klucz CKE: „Wypowiedź jest zatytułowana" = 1 pkt, brak tytułu = 0 pkt za ten element formy.
</div>
