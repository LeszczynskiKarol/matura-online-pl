### Zgodność z poleceniem — elementy treści (5 elementów × max 2 pkt każdy, sumarycznie skalowane do 0-5)

| # | Element | Maks. |
|:-:|---|:-:|

## Wzorcowa odpowiedź — TEMAT 1 (rozprawka, ≈ 235 wyrazów)

<div class="odpowiedz">
  <div>

**Online-only university classes: a leap forward or a step too far?**

The idea of moving all university teaching to a purely online format has gained traction since the pandemic. While such an arrangement offers real benefits, I believe its drawbacks ultimately outweigh its advantages.

On the positive side, online-only studies provide unprecedented flexibility. Students can attend lectures from anywhere, balancing their academic life with work or family responsibilities. Additionally, online learning eliminates commuting costs and reduces the carbon footprint associated with daily travel. Universities can also reach a wider, international audience and provide recordings for revision, which traditionally were available only to those physically present.

However, the disadvantages are equally significant. Firstly, university is not only about acquiring knowledge — it is a formative social experience. Studying exclusively online deprives students of face-to-face debate, networking, and the friendships that often define one's adult life. Secondly, practical subjects such as medicine, chemistry, or engineering require laboratories, equipment, and direct supervision that cannot be replicated digitally. Finally, prolonged screen time has been linked to worsening mental health, reduced concentration, and weaker motivation — problems that disproportionately affect younger students.

To conclude, although online-only universities may seem attractive in theory, they undermine the social, practical, and psychological dimensions of higher education. A hybrid model — combining digital flexibility with in-person teaching — would deliver the best of both worlds without sacrificing what makes university education meaningful in the first place.

  </div>
</div>

## Wzorcowa odpowiedź — TEMAT 2 (list, ≈ 220 wyrazów)

<div class="odpowiedz">
  <div>

```
Dear Editor,

I am writing in response to your recent article on the growing
sense of anonymity in our neighbourhoods. As someone who has lived
in the same block of flats for fifteen years without knowing most
of my neighbours by name, I would like to share both the benefits
of building neighbourly bonds and a concrete proposal for fostering
them.

Stronger neighbourly relationships bring substantial advantages.
Firstly, they create a safety net: in moments of illness, accident
or simply when one is away on holiday, a trusted neighbour can
collect parcels, water plants or call emergency services. Secondly,
they combat loneliness, which has been described by health
authorities as a public-health crisis comparable to smoking.
Finally, communities with strong ties report lower crime rates,
because neighbours notice and report suspicious activity.

To bring residents together, I would like to propose a 'Neighbours'
Weekend' — a Saturday in late May when each block of flats or
street organises a small open-air gathering: a potluck lunch on the
courtyard, a children's chalk-drawing competition and a community
notice board where residents can offer skills or borrow tools. The
event would require only modest funding from the local council,
and could become an annual tradition.

I trust your newspaper will support this idea and help spread it
to other readers.

Yours faithfully,
Karol Leszczyński
```

  </div>

## Strategia: rozprawka (temat 1)

<div class="krok">
  <div class="krok-label">Krok 1 — wstęp z tezą</div>

Teza MUSI być we wstępie i MUSI zapowiadać strukturę. Sygnalizatory: *I believe...*, *In my opinion...*, *I am convinced that...*. Klucz CKE wprost: bez tezy we wstępie max 1 pkt za to kryterium.
</div>

<div class="krok">
  <div class="krok-label">Krok 2 — akapit z argumentami ZA</div>

3-4 argumenty z **konkretami** i **przykładami**. Łączniki: *Firstly... Secondly... Moreover... Additionally...*.
</div>

<div class="krok">
  <div class="krok-label">Krok 3 — akapit z argumentami PRZECIW</div>

3-4 kontrargumenty z konkretami. Łączniki: *However... On the other hand... Conversely... Nevertheless...*.
</div>

<div class="krok">
  <div class="krok-label">Krok 4 — zakończenie z konkluzją</div>

Konkluzja MUSI wynikać z argumentów, nie powtarzać tezy. Sygnalizatory: *To conclude... All things considered... In summary, ...*. Najlepiej: konkluzja kompromisowa (np. „hybrid model" zamiast „online" / „offline").
</div>

## Strategia: list formalny (temat 2)

<div class="krok">
  <div class="krok-label">Krok 1 — zwroty charakterystyczne</div>

Otwarcie: `Dear Sir/Madam,` lub `Dear Editor,`. Zamknięcie: `Yours faithfully,` (jeśli nieznany adresat) lub `Yours sincerely,` (jeśli znany).
</div>

<div class="krok">
  <div class="krok-label">Krok 2 — wstęp z celem listu</div>

`I am writing in response to / with regard to / to express my views on...`. Wstęp musi jasno wskazywać CEL listu.
</div>

<div class="krok">
  <div class="krok-label">Krok 3 — dwa akapity główne</div>

Akapit 1: **korzyści** z relacji z sąsiadami (3-4 konkretne).
Akapit 2: **pomysł na akcję** (`I would like to propose...`) z konkretami — co, gdzie, kiedy, finansowanie.
</div>

<div class="krok">
  <div class="krok-label">Krok 4 — zakończenie</div>

Wezwanie do działania / podziękowanie / wyrażenie nadziei. `I trust your newspaper will support this idea...`.
</div>

## Kluczowe zwroty

```
Wstęp (rozprawka):       The issue of X has recently sparked widespread debate. / Opinions on
                         X are sharply divided. / While X has its merits, I believe...
Wstęp (list):            I am writing in response to / I would like to share my views on...
Argumenty ZA:            Firstly... / Furthermore... / Moreover... / Another key advantage is...
Argumenty PRZECIW:       However... / On the other hand... / Conversely... / Nevertheless...
                         / Critics argue that...
Przykład:                For instance... / A case in point is... / Consider, for example...
Konkluzja (rozprawka):   To conclude... / All things considered... / In summary, while...
                         it would be more sensible to...
Zamknięcie (list):       I trust your newspaper will... / I would appreciate it if...
                         / Yours faithfully, [name]
```

## Najczęstsze błędy uczniów

<div class="pulapka">
  <div class="pulapka-label">Pułapka 1 — brak tezy we wstępie (rozprawka)</div>

Klucz CKE wprost: „W pełni poprawna teza MUSI zapowiadać oczekiwaną strukturę rozprawki. Jeśli teza nie zapowiada oczekiwanej struktury rozprawki, może być zakwalifikowana maksymalnie na poziom 1." Bez tezy = max 1 pkt za to kryterium.
</div>

<div class="pulapka">
  <div class="pulapka-label">Pułapka 2 — sztampowe zakończenie</div>

Zakończenie „In conclusion, online classes have advantages and disadvantages" = SCHEMATYCZNE = max 1 pkt. Klucz CKE wymaga konkluzji „zgodnej z treścią wypowiedzi" — czyli wynikającej z konkretnych argumentów, najlepiej kompromisowej.
</div>

<div class="pulapka">
  <div class="pulapka-label">Pułapka 3 — list bez zwrotu rozpoczynającego</div>

`Hello,` lub samo `Dear,` w liście formalnym = 0 pkt za „Elementy charakterystyczne dla formy". Wymagane: `Dear Sir/Madam,` lub `Dear Editor,`.
</div>

<div class="pulapka">
  <div class="pulapka-label">Pułapka 4 — brak segmentacji</div>

Tekst ciągły bez akapitów = 0 pkt za „Segmentacja". Klucz CKE: „układ graficzny pracy (podział na akapity) jest merytorycznie uzasadniony".
</div>
