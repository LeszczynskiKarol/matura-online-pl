## Wzorcowa odpowiedź — TEMAT 1 (rozprawka, ≈ 240 wyrazów)

<div class="odpowiedz">
  <div>

**Volunteering in the summer holidays — does it pay off?**

Many young people decide to take up some charity work during the summer break instead of taking a paid job or resting. While such a decision has both advantages and disadvantages, I will examine each side before drawing a conclusion.

The benefits are considerable. Firstly, summer voluntary work offers an opportunity to develop soft skills — communication, leadership and teamwork — which are increasingly valued by universities and employers. Secondly, it widens one's horizons: working in an animal shelter or assisting refugees exposes young people to realities they would otherwise never encounter. Finally, voluntary projects often build international friendships that last for decades, especially when the volunteer travels abroad with an organisation such as the European Solidarity Corps.

However, the drawbacks should not be ignored. Most importantly, voluntary work is unpaid, which means young people from lower-income families may be forced to choose paid summer jobs instead, deepening social inequality. Secondly, the holidays are a precious time for rest after an exhausting school year; sacrificing them to demanding charity projects can lead to burn-out before the next school year even begins. Furthermore, some volunteer placements abroad turn out to be poorly organised and leave young people feeling exploited rather than fulfilled.

To conclude, although holiday volunteering brings genuine personal growth, it also entails real costs in terms of money, rest and quality of experience. Young people should consider it carefully and choose well-organised projects that match their goals.

  </div>
</div>

## Wzorcowa odpowiedź — TEMAT 2 (artykuł, ≈ 230 wyrazów)

<div class="odpowiedz">
  <div>

**The race that taught me everything: why every teenager should compete**

Last Saturday I crossed the finish line of my first 30-kilometre mountain bike race — exhausted, mud-splattered and grinning from ear to ear. It was one of the most demanding days of my life, but also the most rewarding.

The race began at dawn in the Kashubian Lake District. Two hundred riders set off in a chaotic cloud of dust along narrow forest paths, and within minutes the leading group had vanished into the trees. I was nowhere near them. My strategy was simple: ride steadily, drink regularly and hope my brakes survived the descents. After two punctures and one spectacular crash into a bramble bush I finally rolled across the finish line — 87th out of 200, but proudly upright.

So why is it worth competing? Firstly, sport teaches you to manage failure. You will lose more races than you win, but every defeat sharpens your motivation. Secondly, sports rivalry builds genuine friendships: rivals on the track often turn into the closest allies off it. Finally, competition reveals your hidden reserves — the moment when your legs say "stop" but your mind says "one more kilometre" is the moment you discover who you really are.

In a world that increasingly rewards comfort, signing up for a race is one of the simplest ways for young people to grow up — and to feel genuinely alive.

  </div>
</div>

## Strategia: rozprawka (temat 1)

<div class="krok">
  <div class="krok-label">Krok 1 — teza zapowiadająca strukturę</div>

Teza MUSI odnosić się do **pracy w ramach wolontariatu w trakcie wakacji** ORAZ zapowiadać, że będą rozpatrywane zalety i wady. Klucz CKE wymaga obu elementów.
</div>

<div class="krok">
  <div class="krok-label">Krok 2 — akapity ZA i PRZECIW</div>

Każdy akapit z 3-4 konkretnymi argumentami + przykładami.
</div>

<div class="krok">
  <div class="krok-label">Krok 3 — konkluzja kompromisowa</div>

Najlepsze konkluzje są **kompromisowe** — wskazują, że obie strony mają rację, ale w pewnych warunkach jedna jest lepsza.
</div>

## Strategia: artykuł (temat 2)

<div class="krok">
  <div class="krok-label">Krok 1 — tytuł</div>

Artykuł MUSI być zatytułowany. Tytuł powinien być chwytliwy, ciekawy, oryginalny.
</div>

<div class="krok">
  <div class="krok-label">Krok 2 — wstęp przyciągający</div>

Pierwszy akapit MUSI „złapać" czytelnika — pytanie retoryczne, anegdota, cytat, surprising fact.
</div>

<div class="krok">
  <div class="krok-label">Krok 3 — relacja z konkretami</div>

Past Simple/Past Continuous. Konkretne miejsce, czas, szczegóły (kilometry, problemy techniczne, klasyfikacja końcowa).
</div>

<div class="krok">
  <div class="krok-label">Krok 4 — wyjaśnienie wartości rywalizacji</div>

3-4 konkretne powody, dlaczego warto. Łączniki: *Firstly... Secondly... Furthermore... Finally...*.
</div>

## Najczęstsze błędy uczniów

<div class="pulapka">
  <div class="pulapka-label">Pułapka 1 — `wolontariat` (po polsku) w tezie</div>

Klucz CKE wprost: użycie wyrazu `wolontariat / volontariat` (polonizm) = **teza niejasna** = max 1 pkt. Trzeba pisać: `volunteering`, `voluntary work`, `charity work`, `work for free`.
</div>

<div class="pulapka">
  <div class="pulapka-label">Pułapka 2 — pomijanie „w trakcie wakacji"</div>

Klucz CKE: teza dotycząca pracy w ramach wolontariatu **bez odniesienia do wakacji** = **teza odbiegająca od tematu**. Przykład klucza: „Volunteering is a great way to meet people, but it also requires a lot of responsibility" = odbiega od tematu (brak wskazania, że to wakacje).
</div>

<div class="pulapka">
  <div class="pulapka-label">Pułapka 3 — `summer job` zamiast wolontariatu</div>

Praca **wakacyjna** ≠ praca wakacyjna w ramach **wolontariatu**. Jeśli argumenty dotyczą pracy płatnej (`summer job`, `internship`), klucz CKE jako **niezgodne z tematem** = 0 pkt za argumenty.
</div>

<div class="pulapka">
  <div class="pulapka-label">Pułapka 4 — artykuł bez tytułu</div>

Klucz CKE wprost: „Wypowiedź jest zatytułowana" = 1 pkt, brak tytułu = 0 pkt za ten element formy.
</div>

## Kluczowe zwroty

```
Wstęp (rozprawka):       Many young people decide to... / The decision to ... has both
                         advantages and disadvantages.
Tytuł (artykuł):          The race that taught me everything / What I learned at...
Wstęp (artykuł):          Last Saturday I... / Have you ever found yourself...
Argumenty ZA:            Firstly... / Furthermore... / Moreover... / Another key advantage...
Argumenty PRZECIW:       However... / On the other hand... / Critics argue that...
Konkluzja:              To conclude... / All things considered... / In summary, ...
```
